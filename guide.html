How RezzCraft works

The short version of everything on the server. In game, Rezz can answer anything this page doesn't: type /rezz and your question.

Getting started

  • New players get a starter kit and starting cash. /checklist pays you for each step of learning the server, and finishing it pays a Vote Key.
  • /rtp takes you somewhere open in the main world. /spawn brings you back. /help and /guide cover the basics.
  • Ask Rezz, our AI helper bot, anything with /rezz and your question. Only you see the answer.
  • Invited by a friend? /refer and their name in your first 48 hours rewards you both. /wherefrom tells us how you found us and gives you a reward too.

Land claims

  • /claimkit gives you the claim tools and /claiminfo explains how claiming works. Claimed land is protected from breaking, building and chest opening.
  • You earn claim blocks by playing, and more from voting, daily rewards, ranks and crates. /claimslist shows your claims and blocks.
  • /trust and a name lets a friend build with you. /accesstrust lets them use doors, buttons and beds only. /untrust removes them.
  • /claimborder shows the edge of the claim you are standing in. /abandonclaim removes a claim and gives the blocks back.
  • You can claim in the main world, the nether and the End. The resource and creative worlds can't be claimed.

Homes and teleporting

  • /sethome saves a home and /home takes you there. Earned and store ranks give you more homes.
  • /tpa and a name asks to teleport to a friend. /tpahere asks them to come to you. They answer with /tpaccept or /tpdeny.
  • /warp opens the warp menu with the resource, creative and hardcore worlds.

Money and trading

  • /sell opens a menu where you sell items to the server, and /shop shows what the server buys and sells.
  • Make your own chest shop with /chestshop create while looking at a chest. /chestshop browse lists every player shop.
  • /ah opens the auction house. /orders lets you post buy orders for items you want.
  • Rent a shop or a hotel room at spawn by right-clicking a rent sign.
  • Hire other players with contracts: /contract create posts a job, and the pay is held safely until the job is done.
  • /bal shows your money, /pay sends money, and /baltop shows the richest players.

Crates, voting and daily rewards

  • The crates are just east of spawn. Hold the matching key and right-click a crate to open it. Left-click to see what's inside.
  • Keys come from voting, the daily login streak (/daily), the starter checklist and ranking up. /cratekeys buys more with in-game money.
  • Every vote pays cash, claim blocks and a Vote Key. When the whole server reaches a vote goal, everyone online gets a vote party bonus.

The worlds

  • Main world, nether and End: where you build. Claims, homes and teleports work here, and PvP is off.
  • Resource world (/warp resource): mine and gather without touching the main world. It shares your inventory. PvP is on here.
  • Creative world (/warp creative): build freely with a separate inventory. Nothing comes back to survival.
  • Hardcore world (/warp hardcore): separate inventory and PvP on. Die and you become a spectator until you earn your way back in with XP.

PvP, duels and the arena

  • /ffa drops you into the free-for-all arena with a kit. Your own items are kept safe and nothing is lost when you die there.
  • /duel and a name challenges a player, with a kit or your own gear. /queue is ranked matchmaking with a rating.
  • Outside the arena, PvP is on only in the resource and hardcore worlds.

Stargates

  • Players build their own stargates out of obsidian and a lever, then dial each other by name. /stargatebook has the build diagrams.
  • Public gates connect spawn, the main stronghold and the End.

Skills and fishing

  • Skills like mining, woodcutting and combat level up as you play. /stats shows yours and /mctop shows the leaderboards.
  • Any fishing rod can catch custom fish of different rarities. /emf shop sells them, and fishing contests start for everyone automatically.

Handy extras

  • /ec opens your ender chest anywhere. /trash throws items away. /sort turns on chest sorting.
  • The server has optional proximity voice chat: hear players near you. It needs a free mod; ask Rezz or Discord for the link.
  • /map gives you the link to the live 3D map, which works on phone and PC.
  • /msg sends a private message, /mail leaves one for someone offline, and /ignore hides a player's chat from you.
