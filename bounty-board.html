New at spawn

The Bounty Board

Daily quests, rotating bounties and rewards you can't buy. Right-click the NPC at spawn, or type /bounties anywhere.

What's on the board

Always something to do

Daily Quests

Three new quests every day. Mining, kills and fishing count on their own while you play. Finish all three for a Vote Key.

Bounties

Bigger jobs that everyone sees, with new ones through the day. Each pays cash and a Bounty Scroll you can spend on more quests.

Rare Bounties

They show up now and then, announced in chat. The first player to finish wins a Jackpot Key.

Questlines

Eight long quests, step by step. Each step pays, and the last pays most. The hardest ones earn exclusive titles.

Head Hunts

Bring in the head of an Elder Guardian, Ender Dragon, Wither or Warden that you collected yourself, and win a trophy and a title.

Hunter Levels

Everything you finish raises your Hunter Level from 1 to 5, and each level unlocks more, including weekly contracts at the top.

What you can earn

Earned, never sold

Eight trophies to display in your own claim, nine titles to wear after your name, and the Golden Balloon, which comes with the Gold Trophy. None of them are in the store.

Find it

Where to go

Walk straight west from /spawn, about 50 blocks, or head to -73 67 84 in the overworld. Hand-ins happen at the board.
