Ranks

Ranks you earn, not buy

Climb nine ranks with in-game money and time played, then keep going with prestige. Every rank-up also pays crate keys.

RankIn-game moneyTime playedUnlocks
Wanderer$15,00010 h2 homes
Settler$35,00025 h/hat, bigger ender chest, bonus claim blocks
Trader$75,00060 hSell-all and sell-hand commands, fish sell-all
Artisan$175,000120 h3 homes, instant teleports, /head
Explorer$400,000200 h4 homes, /craft, no /rtp wait, bonus claim blocks
Pioneer$800,000300 h5 homes, biggest ender chest, no teleport cooldown
Vanguard$2.5M450 h6 homes, /back (even after death), bonus claim blocks
Warden$5M650 h8 homes, a personal base warp anyone can /visit
Sovereign$10M1,000 h10 homes, a spot on /legends, unlocks prestige

Type /rankup in game to see your progress. Time played is your total on the server. After Sovereign, /prestige adds a star to your name each time.

Supporting the server

Store ranks are cosmetic and convenience only

Supporter ranks and cosmetics help pay for the server. They add things like name tags, particles and handy commands. They never sell gear, game money or claim blocks.

Everything in the store is optional, and the game is fully playable without it.
