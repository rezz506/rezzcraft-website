Rules

Five rules, one idea: be a good neighbor

These keep RezzCraft a place people want to come back to.

  1. Be respectful

    No harassment, bullying, racism or toxicity of any kind.

  2. No politics or controversial topics

    Keep chat about Minecraft. Zero tolerance.

  3. No hacking

    Hacked clients or cheats mean an immediate permanent ban.

  4. No griefing, base camping or forced PvP

    Don't steal from or destroy anyone's builds. PvP has its own places: the arena, duels, the resource world and the hardcore world.

  5. Don't abuse bugs or glitches

    Found one? Report it with /ticket in game, in our Discord, or at support@rezzcraft.us.
