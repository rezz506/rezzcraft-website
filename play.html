How to join

Join RezzCraft in under a minute

Same world for Java and Bedrock. Copy the address, add the server, and you are in.

Server addressChecking status
mc.rezzcraft.us
Bedrock (phone, Windows, console): same address, port 19132.

Java Edition

  1. Open Minecraft and click Multiplayer.
  2. Click Add Server.
  3. Server address: mc.rezzcraft.us, then click Done.
  4. Pick RezzCraft and click Join Server.

Bedrock Edition

  1. Open Minecraft and tap Play, then the Servers tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add Server.
  3. Name: RezzCraft. Address: mc.rezzcraft.us. Port: 19132.
  4. Save, then tap RezzCraft to join.

On Xbox, PlayStation or Switch, adding your own servers takes a few extra steps. Ask in Discord and we will walk you through it.

Questions

Common questions

Is RezzCraft free?

Yes. RezzCraft is free to join and play. The store is optional and only sells cosmetics and convenience perks.

Can I play on Bedrock?

Yes. Java and Bedrock players share the same world. On phones and Windows, add the server with the address mc.rezzcraft.us and port 19132. On Xbox, PlayStation and Switch, adding your own servers takes a few extra steps, so ask in our Discord and we will help.

My phone will not connect on mobile data. What do I do?

If tapping Join only shows a screen with Back and Play at the top, turn on the Minecraft setting Allow mobile data for servers, then try again. Join with the address rezzcraft.us and port 19132.

Is RezzCraft pay-to-win?

No. Every gameplay rank is earned by playing. Store ranks add cosmetics and convenience perks and never sell gear, game money or claim blocks.

Can my base get griefed?

Not if you claim it. Claimed land is protected from breaking, building and opening chests. Griefing is against the rules everywhere.

Is there PvP?

PvP is off in the main world, nether and End. You can fight in the free-for-all arena, duels and ranked queues, and PvP is on in the resource world and the hardcore world.

Which Minecraft version should I use?

Join with the latest version of Minecraft Java or Bedrock.

Do I need mods?

No. RezzCraft works with the normal game. Proximity voice chat is optional and needs a free mod; ask Rezz or our Discord for the link.

How do I get help in game?

Type /rezz and your question to ask Rezz, our AI helper bot. For problems like griefing or bugs, type /ticket and staff will answer. You can also email support@rezzcraft.us.
