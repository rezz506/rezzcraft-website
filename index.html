What's here

Survival the way you remember it, with a few good extras

Vanilla at heart. Everything we add is there to protect your work, reward your time or give you someone to play with.

LAND

Your builds stay yours

Claim your land and nobody can break, open or take anything inside. Trust friends to build with you.

$

A real player economy

Sell to the server, run your own chest shops, list items on the auction house, post buy orders, or hire players with contracts.

RANK

Ranks you earn by playing

Nine ranks plus prestige, all earned with in-game money and time played. New homes, perks and titles as you climb.

KEY

Crates and daily rewards

Vote, log in daily and finish the starter checklist for crate keys, cash and claim blocks. Vote parties pay everyone online.

GATE

Build your own stargates

Players build portal gates and dial each other across the map. Public gates link spawn, the stronghold and the End.

XP

Skills, fishing and contests

Level up skills like mining and woodcutting as you play. Catch custom fish of every rarity and win fishing contests.

PVP

PvP when you want it

PvP is off in the main world. Fight in the free-for-all arena, ranked duels, the resource world or the hardcore world.

5W

Five worlds to play

The main world, nether and End, a resource world for mining, a creative world, and a hardcore world for the brave.

?

Help whenever you need it

Rezz, our AI helper bot, answers questions in chat any time. Real staff and friendly regulars are around too.

Your first hour

New here? You start with a head start

New players get a starter kit and some cash, and the starter checklist pays you for learning the ropes.

Find your spot

Type /rtp to land somewhere open, then /sethome so you can always get back.

Claim it

/claimkit gives you the claim tools and /claiminfo shows how. Claimed land is protected.

Get paid to learn

/checklist pays you for each step, and finishing it pays a Vote Key.

Bring a friend

Invited by a friend? Type /refer and their name in your first 48 hours and you both get rewards.

Since 2021

A small server that has been around a while

The current RezzCraft world has been running since 2021, and the first RezzCraft opened in 2017. It's a place where people know your name, staff answer tickets, and your base is still there when you come back.
