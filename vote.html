Vote daily, get paid daily

Each vote pays in-game cash, claim blocks and a Vote Key. When the server hits its vote goal, everyone online gets a vote party bonus. Voting is also the best way to help new players find us.

Use your exact Minecraft name when you vote. Bedrock players: include the dot at the start of your name.
